# PyDact

PyDact is a privacy tool designed to automatically detect and redact sensitive information. It runs entirely on your device, keeping your data local.

## Goals

- Automatically identify and redact sensitive information.
- Support a wide range of devices, including those with limited processing power.
- Provide reliable AI-assisted detection and redaction.

## Current Status

PyDact is in early development:

- All processing runs locally.
- Resource requirements are currently high, particularly RAM usage.
- AI features are experimental and may miss sensitive information or flag content incorrectly. Always review the results before sharing redacted material.
